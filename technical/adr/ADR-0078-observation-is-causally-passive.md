## Decision

Observation is causally passive and may read, project, transport, buffer, aggregate, visualize and record, but has no authority to modify observed causal state. Pause, step, resume, speed control and runtime manipulation are OPERATOR CONTROL or, where applicable, EXPERIMENTAL INTERVENTION, regardless of process/API/UI co-location. Evaluate informational and computational passivity separately; cost alone is not a violation, material causal-trajectory alteration is.
