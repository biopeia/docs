## Decision

A canonical longitudinal Organism has at most one non-terminated continuation under one authoritative identity. Dormancy remains a non-terminated continuation. Restore/re-embodiment may continue it; every non-canonical fork/copy/branch receives a new identity. Restoring an older checkpoint while a later continuation remains active is a fork unless the later continuation is explicitly retired first; a retired continuation cannot resume as that same identity. Canonical-continuation designation must be explicit and recorded; its owner/storage topology is reserved to Pass 15 and must not depend on Lab. Experimental replication from a checkpoint is legitimate when explicitly designated as intervention; every arm/repetition receives its own identity and provenance, and is not canonical unless explicitly designated. Branches share ancestry/provenance but are not offspring, do not advance generation, and are measured as a distinct branch relation. Reduced experimental subjects must declare their identity regime.
