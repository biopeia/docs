## Decision

**Somatic state ownership: body holds, Organism regulates and feels.** A body instance owns its energy reserve/capacity, structural integrity, accumulated damage, age, senescence and viability; these end with that body and are neither longitudinal Organism identity nor transferred on re-embodiment. Organism owns longitudinal set points, drives/priorities, learned needs-model, deprivation/damage/recovery memory, and maintenance-versus-reproduction allocation; these survive body change, are preserved by canonical re-embodiment, and are recalibrated against the new body. The allocation rule may be heritable under the accepted germline rules. Somatic state reaches Organism only through body somatic state → interoceptive transduction → opaque Organism signal, calibrated per embodiment episode. Consequences (damage, absorption and mechanical-work cost) apply to the body; all embodied expenditure, including cognition, is charged to body somatic state. Organism originates reproduction; the parent's body pays its cost; offspring receives its own body and somatic state. Body-bound motor competence and sensory/interoceptive calibration are not transferred and must be reacquired. Dormancy without active embodiment has progressive non-zero longitudinal-state loss and a defined causal-time limit beyond which Organism death occurs; numerical parameters are experimental calibration, not constitutional values. Re-embodiment is not guaranteed and requires an ordinarily available World body; Lab-provided body is an explicit recorded experimental intervention. Scientific measures must name the AD-0014 lifecycle event they count. This decision does not determine body-implementation authority, World shape/physics-step ownership, per-kind metabolic reserve ownership, stress-acquired epigenetic ownership, interoceptive noise/reliability, numeric calibration, or channel implementation.
