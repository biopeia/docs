## Decision

Scientific provenance, historically relevant evidence and artifacts required for reproducibility or longitudinal interpretation may require preservation. Preserving scientific meaning does not impose backward compatibility on target code, schemas, APIs or runtimes. No item-level retention/disposition is ratified here.
