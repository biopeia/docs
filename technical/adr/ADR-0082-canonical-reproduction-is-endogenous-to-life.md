## Decision

Canonical ecosystem reproduction is endogenous to life, potentially emerging from Organism capacity/state, World coordination, Environment conditions, embodiment constraints and inter-organism interaction. Lab-triggered maturity/birth is not endogenous reproduction; Lab may cause/modify reproduction only as an explicit experimental intervention. Keep reproduction mechanism, endogenous reproduction, population dynamics, selection and evolution distinct.
