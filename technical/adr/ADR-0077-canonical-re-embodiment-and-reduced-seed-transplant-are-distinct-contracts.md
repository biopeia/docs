- **Type:** CONSTITUTIONAL NORMATIVE

## Decision

Preserve `CANONICAL_REEMBODIMENT` and `REDUCED_SEED_TRANSPLANT` as distinct longitudinal contracts in the current-code audit; do not transfer conclusions or imply equivalence across their runtime paths. Whether persistent clean World may use reduced-seed semantics for its intended claims is a separate owner decision.
