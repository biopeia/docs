## Decision

For ordinary TARGET reproduction, ORGANISM is the originating/endogenous reproductive authority: internal physiological capacity/state originates the transition. WORLD may coordinate, resolve, validate conditions/resources/conflict, choreograph offspring registration, and commit, but may not invent readiness, force ordinary maturity, or choose reproduction as an external goal. ENVIRONMENT supplies conditions/resources/hazards/ecological constraints/opportunities; EMBODIMENT supplies viability/somatic/resource constraints; neither originates the authority. Inter-organism interaction may contribute conditions without transferring authority. LAB has no ordinary causal reproductive role; causal manipulation is only an explicitly recorded EXPERIMENTAL INTERVENTION. Read ADR-0033 as the Organism-owned trigger/transition authority and AD-0008 as outcome dependence on World coordination and environmental/embodiment/interaction conditions.
