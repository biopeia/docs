## Decision

Canonical Organism is longitudinal; identity survives body, embodiment-session, world-presence, runtime-instance and checkpoint changes when the operation continues that same Organism. Preserve contract-relevant identity/experience state (including learned state, memory, private models, cognitive development, agency, competence, identity metadata and permitted inherited/epigenetic state); exclude body-instance-only, presentation, cache, environmental truth and temporary runtime machinery. `CANONICAL_REEMBODIMENT` preserves same-Organism continuity and does not silently reset cognition. `REDUCED_SEED_TRANSPLANT` is not equivalent and may only be represented explicitly as a reduced experimental subject/apparatus/initialization. No backward-compatibility obligation; KEEP/REWRITE/DELETE/ARCHIVE is evidence-based.
