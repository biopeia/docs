## Decision

World lifecycle is independent from Lab. Lab may create/configure/attach/observe/intervene explicitly/collect evidence/detach/terminate under explicit control. A started World may continue without Lab when execution mode permits; Workbench, Observatory, UI or analysis-process disappearance is not automatically World death or invalidation.
