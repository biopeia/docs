## Decision

The Organism owns the semantic meaning and authority of its private longitudinal state (including memory, learning, private models, cognitive development, agency, competence and identity-bearing state). World, Lab, Observation and host do not acquire semantic ownership by coordinating, executing, observing or persisting it. World may coordinate atomicity/snapshots/recovery but may not interpret or selectively rewrite private internals, or treat deep copies as its semantic domain state. Internal Organism conditions are exposed outward as Organism-emitted signals/actions, not obtained by another authority reading private internals. Delegated host/Lab execution (e.g. private-model training) remains under Organism authority for request, corpus, adoption and promotion. Observation/Lab projections do not confer write or semantic ownership. Prefer an opaque Organism-defined checkpoint/restore contract coordinated by World. Somatic state, longitudinal regulation and interoceptive boundary are additionally specified by AD-0020; remaining body-schema/sensory-adaptation details and exact implementation stay subject to Pass 15. No Thin/Hybrid/Full World choice implied.
